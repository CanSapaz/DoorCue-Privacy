DoorCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

DoorCue ("the app") reminds you of items you might forget when leaving home, using Wi-Fi, NFC and Bluetooth triggers. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data never leaves your device — the only network request the app makes is an anonymous weather lookup, and only if you keep that feature enabled.

Data the app stores (on your device only)

  • Profiles, item lists and departure history you create inside the app.
  • Voice recordings you make for the own-voice reminder feature.
  • Proof photos you take from the checklist.
  • Your home Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and the identifiers of NFC/BLE tags you assign.
  • Settings (theme, language, triggers, quiet hours).

All of it is deleted permanently when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Data we collect

None. DoorCue has no backend servers, no accounts, no analytics, no trackers and no advertising SDKs. We cannot see your lists, recordings, photos or location.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Location: Android requires location permission to read Wi-Fi network names, which powers the "left home Wi-Fi" trigger. Your location is never tracked, stored or shared. If weather suggestions are enabled, your approximate coordinates are sent anonymously to the Open-Meteo API solely to fetch today's forecast and are not retained.
  • Bluetooth: used for the Bluetooth trigger and for verifying that BLE-tagged items are nearby.
  • NFC: used to open your checklist when you tap the tag on your door.
  • Microphone: used only while you record a voice reminder.
  • Camera: used only while you take a proof photo.
  • Notifications: used for departure and return reminders.

Third parties

  • Open-Meteo: weather forecasts use the free Open-Meteo API (only when the weather feature is enabled; nothing beyond approximate coordinates is sent). No other third-party service is used.

Data deletion

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
